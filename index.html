Pitch

Problem

Many Nigerians hear about Bitcoin but have no simple safe place to learn it and try a wallet.

How it works

LearnSats is one loop. Read a short lesson. Take a 3 question quiz. Pass and earn 100 demo sats. Then practise receiving and sending those demo sats in the built in demo wallet. When you are ready, open the Real Wallet tab to create a real Signet testnet wallet and try a real testnet transaction. Ask Sats, the on-device tutor, helps in simple English.

Who it helps

Students like Ada in Ibadan who want to understand Bitcoin before they use it. Market traders like Chidi who want fast small payments without cash. Families who receive money from relatives abroad and want to understand the options safely.

What is demo

Everything with money in the lessons and the demo wallet is demo. Demo sats are not real BTC. No real payment is made there, and no real transaction is created. The Real Wallet tab is different: it is a real Bitcoin Signet testnet wallet, with a real address, a real balance read from the Esplora API, and real testnet sending. Its coins are test coins, worth nothing.
