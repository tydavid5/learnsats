Pitch

Problem

Many Nigerians hear about Bitcoin but have no simple safe place to learn it and try a wallet.

How it works

LearnSats is one loop. Read a short lesson. Take a 3 question quiz. Pass and earn 100 demo sats. Then practise receiving and sending those demo sats in the built in demo wallet. Ask Sats, the on-device tutor, helps in simple English.

Who it helps

Students like Ada in Ibadan who want to understand Bitcoin before they use it. Market traders like Chidi who want fast small payments without cash. Families who receive money from relatives abroad and want to understand the options safely.

What is demo

Everything with money here is demo. Demo sats are not real BTC. The wallet is not connected to Bitcoin or Lightning. No real payment is made, and no real transaction is created.
